# AK-47 source

Author: Vinrax. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/ak-47-low-poly

License: CC-BY 3.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Original archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Ak47.zip

Original OBJ and MTL are retained with the author's readme. The archive is untextured; the MTL mentions an unavailable image. The adaptation replaces those materials, normalizes geometry to 0.87 m length, adds a two-bone rig and draft animation clips, and exports GLB. Current materials and animations are drafts; first-person hands and texture work are outstanding.
